#TopoDroid v. 6.5.47 - What's new
 
1. Dropped Spanish and Portuguese translations.
2. Revised Therion export: shot comment on the shot line.
   Therion does not handle shot notes. To allow these to be exported and subsequently easily imported, we have changed the comment format.
3. Therion parser revised.
   Importing measurement notes.
4. Size-reduction of man page images.
   Images with reduced color usage that take up less space yet offer better resolution.
5. New setting: ZIP auto-save whenever survey data window is closed.
6. Survey/TdConfig lck-file mechanism (currently empty).
