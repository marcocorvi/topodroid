#TopoDroid v. 6.5.44 - What's new
 
1. LOCATION permission is non-mandatory.
   The mandatory permissions are BT-CONNECT and STORAGE-ACCESS (READ / WRITE).
2. LOCATION, CAMERA, and AUDIO permissions are requested when needed.
   For example, when you start a BLE scan, the app requests the LOCATION permission
   if not already granted. Likewise when you take a x-section or a shot photo, the
   CAMEA permission is requested, if not already granted.
3. Shapefile export with "prj" files.
   Only for WGS84 and UTM coordinate systems.
   If the georeferenced point coordinates are not converted to a cartografic 
   coordinate system, the export writes prj files for WGS84. It the coordinates have
   been converted to a UTM coordinate system the app writes the prj files for that 
   coordinate system. The app uses the coordinate system name to identify the system,
   eg, UTM32N or UTM09S. Alternate names, such as EPSG:32632 and EPSG:32709, are also
   allowed.
